BMI and waist-to-height calculator for adults

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². For most adults a healthy BMI is 18.5–24.9. For people with a South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African or African-Caribbean background, NICE uses lower limits: overweight from 23 and obesity from 27.5. Your waist should be less than half your height.

Who this is for: adults aged 18 and over. Do not use it if you are pregnant, if you have or think you may have an eating disorder, or if you have a condition that affects your height (NHS advice). BMI cannot tell muscle from fat, so it can read high in very muscular people. NICE also says to read BMI with care from age 65.

Waist-to-height ratio (NICE, adults with BMI under 35)

RatioMeaning
0.4–0.49no increased health risk from your waist size.
0.5–0.59increased health risks. Your waist is half your height or more.
0.6+further increased health risks. Talk to a doctor or nurse.

Example: 70 kg and 1.70 m → 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 24.2. A 85 cm waist at 170 cm height → 85 ÷ 170 = 0.50.

How to measure your waist

Find the bottom of your ribs and the top of your hips. Wrap a tape measure around your waist halfway between them — this is usually just above the belly button. Breathe out normally, then measure (NICE).
