What we do

Teaching, preparation and practical tools

IKJON is an education and consulting centre. Our main focus is getting students ready for university or work.

01

English and Ona tili

General English from beginner to advanced level, Kids English, and Ona tili lessons.

02

Certificates and guidance

IELTS and CEFR preparation, plus advice on choosing a university or a career path.

03

Sport and health tools

Free apps and guides for football training, daily fitness and injury recovery, based on published research.

What we value

How we work

Evidence first

Our lessons and health plans follow recognised standards and published research.

Honesty

We are clear about what our tools can do. They do not replace a teacher, doctor or physiotherapist.

Privacy

Our free apps need no account and do not track you. Your data stays on your device.

Open to everyone

Our sport and health tools are free and available in Uzbek, Russian and English.
