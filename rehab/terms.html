4. Your safety

  • See a doctor before starting if you have a recent injury, recent surgery, a heart or lung condition, high blood pressure, are pregnant, or are unsure whether exercise is safe for you.
  • Seek urgent medical help if you notice any warning sign listed in the app, such as being unable to bear weight, numbness, a deformed joint, fever with a hot swollen joint, or loss of bladder or bowel control.
  • Stop an exercise if pain is sharp or goes above 5/10, if you feel dizzy, short of breath or unwell, or if swelling increases afterwards.
  • After surgery, always follow your surgeon's and physiotherapist's instructions over the app.
