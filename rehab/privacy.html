Privacy Policy

IKJON Joint Rehab · Effective 1 November 2026

In short: If you use the free app, we collect nothing: no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and everything you enter stays on your device. If you buy Premium, our payment partner Lemon Squeezy handles your payment, and we receive only the order details we need to provide Premium and support you. Your plan and pain diary never leave your device, even with Premium.

1. Who we are

IKJON Joint Rehab ("the app") is provided by IKJON Consulting & Education Centre, Uzbekistan ("IKJON", "we", "us"). Contact: ikjongearbox@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Free use: we do not collect any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts, and the app does not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools.

If you buy Premium: see sections 5 and 6 below.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, it saves the following in your browser's local storage on your own device only:

  • the plan you choose (injury or programme, start date, plan length, days completed and ticked tasks);
  • your session log (date, pain score from 0–10, how hard the session felt, your optional next-morning check and any notes you type);
  • that you accepted the Terms of Use, and the date;
  • if you have Premium: your licence key, a random device ID created when you activate it, and whether the licence is active.

Your plan, session log and notes are never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it for you. Pain scores and notes are health-related, so we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

4. Technical data when the app loads

When you open or update the app, your device downloads the app's files from our web host. Like any website, the hosting provider may automatically process basic technical data (such as your IP address and browser type) in its server logs so it can deliver the files securely. We do not use this data to identify you, and we do not combine it with anything else. All exercise photos, the logo and fonts are packaged with the app. The only other website the app contacts is Lemon Squeezy's licence server, and only if you use Premium (see section 6).

5. Buying Premium

Premium is sold by our payment partner and reseller Lemon Squeezy (Link, LLC, USA), which acts as the "merchant of record". When you buy, you enter your details on Lemon Squeezy's checkout, not in our app. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email address, billing country and payment details and processes them under its own Privacy Policy. We never see or store your card details.

Lemon Squeezy shares with us: your name, email address, country, what you bought, the price paid, order and subscription status, and your licence key. We use this only to:

  • provide Premium and your licence key;
  • answer your questions and handle cancellations and refunds;
  • prevent fraud and misuse of licence keys;
  • keep the records that tax and accounting laws require.

We do not use it for advertising and we do not send you marketing emails unless you separately ask us to. We keep order records for as long as you have Premium and afterwards only as long as tax and accounting laws require, and then delete them.

6. Licence checks

When you activate a licence key, and about every 3 days afterwards while you are online, the app sends your licence key and the random device ID to Lemon Squeezy's licence server (api.lemonsqueezy.com) to confirm the licence is valid. Like any internet connection, this also shares your IP address with Lemon Squeezy. Nothing else is sent: not your plan, pain scores, notes or any health information. Lemon Squeezy's reply includes your name and email, but the app ignores and does not save them. If you choose "Remove from this device", the app tells Lemon Squeezy to release that device and deletes the key from your device.

7. International transfers

Lemon Squeezy is based in the United States, so purchase and licence information is processed there. Lemon Squeezy is responsible for protecting it under its Privacy Policy and the safeguards required by law.

8. Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google processes information about your download under the Google Privacy Policy. We only receive anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google (for example, the number of installs). We receive no personal information from Google.

9. Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

10. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share purchase information only with Lemon Squeezy and its payment providers to process your order, and with authorities only if the law requires it.

11. Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over. Young people under 16 should use it together with a parent or guardian. Premium must be bought by an adult (18+), or by a young person with their parent's or guardian's permission. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Your choices and control

  • Export: In Privacy & settings you can save a copy of your plan and log.
  • Delete: In Privacy & settings you can delete all app data on this device at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data, also deletes everything on your device.
  • Premium: you can cancel your subscription at any time using the link in your receipt email or by contacting us. To move Premium to another device, choose "Remove from this device" first.

13. Security

Because your plan and pain diary never leave your device, they cannot be exposed by a breach of our systems. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, so card details never reach us. We protect order records with strong passwords and two-step sign-in, and only IKJON staff who need them can see them. The app is served over a secure, encrypted connection (HTTPS).

14. Your rights

Under the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data" and similar laws elsewhere (such as the GDPR in the EU and UK), you have rights to know what personal data is processed about you, and to access, correct or delete it, or object to its use. If you use only the free app, we hold no personal data about you. If you bought Premium, contact us and we will provide, correct or delete your order information, except records we must keep by law. For data held by Lemon Squeezy, you can also contact Lemon Squeezy directly. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

15. Changes to this policy

If we add features that need more personal data (for example accounts), we will update this policy before the feature launches, explain exactly what is collected and why, and ask for your permission where the law requires it. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
