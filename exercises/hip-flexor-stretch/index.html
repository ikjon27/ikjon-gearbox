Hip flexor stretch: how to do it

Works: Hip front flexibility. Type: Stretch — held positions for flexibility.

Written by: IKJON teamLast checked: 9 November 2026How we write health pages →

How to do it

  1. Half-kneel, back knee on a cushion.
  2. Tuck the pelvis under and squeeze the back glute.
  3. Shift forward gently.

Coaching tips

Breathe slowly; no bouncing.

SpeedEase in slowly, then hold still
EffortMild pull — about 4/10, never sharp
BreathingSlow breaths; relax more as you breathe out
What you should feelA gentle stretch that fades a little as you hold

Equipment

Cushion

Safety: stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness or chest pain. If you are recovering from an injury or surgery, follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's advice first.
