Drop landing: how to do it

Works: Landing control. Type: Plyometric — hops, jumps and landings for sport.

Written by: IKJON teamLast checked: 9 November 2026How we write health pages →

Target muscles

Main muscles: Quadriceps, Glutes

Also working: Calves, Hamstrings

How to do it

  1. Step off a low box (20–30 cm).
  2. Land on both feet softly, hips back, knees over toes.
  3. Hold the landing 2 s.

Coaching tips

Quiet landings mean good shock absorption.

SpeedQuick and springy, land softly
EffortHard — quality over quantity
BreathingBreathe out on each jump
What you should feelSpringy legs; stop when landings get noisy or knees cave in

Equipment

Box

Safety: stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness or chest pain. If you are recovering from an injury or surgery, follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's advice first.
