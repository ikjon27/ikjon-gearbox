Side plank: how to do it

Works: Obliques, side of the trunk. Type: Core — trunk control and endurance.

Written by: IKJON teamLast checked: 9 November 2026How we write health pages →

How to do it

  1. On your side, elbow under the shoulder.
  2. Knees bent first, then straight legs.
  3. Lift hips into a line and hold.

Coaching tips

Head, hips, feet in one line.

SpeedSlow and controlled; keep the spine still
EffortMedium — 5–7/10
BreathingSteady breathing, ribs down
What you should feelTummy and trunk working, not the low back

Equipment

None — your body weight

Safety: stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness or chest pain. If you are recovering from an injury or surgery, follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's advice first.
