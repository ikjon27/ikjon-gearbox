Seated upper-back extension: how to do it

Works: Upper back mobility. Type: Mobility — gentle movement to restore range.

Written by: IKJON teamLast checked: 9 November 2026How we write health pages →

How to do it

  1. Sit on a chair, hands behind your head.
  2. Gently arch the upper back over the chair back, looking up.
  3. Return to upright.

Coaching tips

Move from the upper back, not the low back.

SpeedSlow and smooth, 2–3 s each way
EffortEasy — about 3/10
BreathingBreathe normally
What you should feelGentle movement, no pain

Equipment

Chair

Safety: stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness or chest pain. If you are recovering from an injury or surgery, follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's advice first.
