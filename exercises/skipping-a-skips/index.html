Skipping (A-skips): how to do it

Works: Running coordination, calf spring. Type: Cardio — heart and lung fitness.

Written by: IKJON teamLast checked: 9 November 2026How we write health pages →

How to do it

  1. Skip forward driving one knee up.
  2. Land on the ball of the foot.
  3. Swing the arms opposite to the legs.

Coaching tips

Great warm-up before running and sprinting.

SpeedSteady pace you can keep up
EffortModerate — you can talk but not sing
BreathingRhythmic breathing
What you should feelWarm and slightly out of breath

Equipment

None — your body weight

Safety: stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness or chest pain. If you are recovering from an injury or surgery, follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's advice first.
