Single-leg calf raise: how to do it

Works: Calves, Achilles. Type: Strength — moving against load to rebuild muscle & tendon.

Written by: IKJON teamLast checked: 9 November 2026How we write health pages →

How to do it

  1. Stand on one foot, light hand support.
  2. Rise as high as possible.
  3. Lower over 3 s.

Coaching tips

Healthy target: 25+ reps each side.

Speed2–3 s down, short pause, 1–2 s up
EffortHard but controlled — stop with 2 reps left
BreathingBreathe out as you push or lift
What you should feelMuscle burn is fine; joint pain should stay 3/10 or less

Equipment

None — your body weight

Safety: stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness, numbness or chest pain. If you are recovering from an injury or surgery, follow your doctor's or physiotherapist's advice first.
