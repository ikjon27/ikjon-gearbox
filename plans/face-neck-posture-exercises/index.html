Face, Neck & Posture Exercises: 30-Day Plan

Stand taller and look fresher: face and jaw exercises, eye breaks, neck and upper-back strength, plus sun protection and sleep habits.

Daily care routine

Each day: two face exercises, one for the eyes, one for the neck and a scalp massage.

Hair & scalp: Scalp massage

Face & jaw: Cheek lifter, Jaw relax & open, Forehead smoother, Chin & neck lift, Smile hold

Eyes: Near–far focus, Eye movements, Palming

Neck & posture: Chin tuck, Neck & upper-shoulder release, Wall slide, Neck mobility

Daily habits

  • Sunscreen SPF 30+ on face and neck
  • Drink water through the day (about 6–8 glasses)
  • 20-20-20 eye breaks while on screens
  • Posture check every hour: chin back, shoulders down
  • Brush teeth morning and night (2 min)
  • Sleep 7–9 hours

How to progress

Start at a level that feels easy to medium. When every set feels easy, add 1–2 reps or one more set. If you miss a day, simply continue — the plan does not reset.

Before you start: if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, are pregnant, or have a recent injury or surgery, check with a doctor first. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or chest pain.
